# Module 10: Copyright & Intellectual Property ⚖️

**Week 10 | Legal Frameworks for Information**

---

## The Big Picture

Can you share a meme on social media? Download a song? Use an image in a presentation? Can an AI company train its models on millions of books? The answers depend on copyright law: a system designed for a pre-digital world that courts have spent the last few years dramatically reinterpreting for the age of AI.

This week, we explore intellectual property, copyright, and net neutrality, two areas of law that have both produced landmark decisions very recently.

### 🎯 What You'll Learn

- Understand the basics of copyright and intellectual property
- Learn about fair use and when you can use copyrighted material
- Follow the rise and fall of net neutrality as a case study in how tech law gets made (and unmade)
- Learn what courts have actually decided about AI and copyright
- Evaluate the ethics of information ownership

### 🧠 Big Questions to Consider

- Who owns information in the digital age?
- Is copyright law fit for the internet era?
- Should all internet traffic be treated equally, and who gets to decide?
- Who owns AI-generated content, and was it legal to build the AI in the first place?
