# Module 12: Artificial Intelligence 🤖

**Week 12 | The AI Revolution**

---

## The Big Picture

Artificial Intelligence is transforming every aspect of our lives, from how we search for information to how we receive medical diagnoses. AI chatbots, image generators, and AI agents have moved from science fiction to everyday tools. But with great power comes great responsibility.

This module explores AI: what it is, how it works, and what it means for our future.

### 🎯 What You'll Learn

- Understand what AI is and how it has evolved
- Learn the basics of machine learning and neural networks
- Explore current AI applications and their implications
- Understand the emerging legal and regulatory landscape for AI
- Consider the ethical challenges of AI development

### 🧠 Big Questions to Consider

- What can AI do well? What can't it do?
- How do we ensure AI systems are fair and unbiased?
- What jobs will AI change or replace?
- Who is responsible for AI's mistakes?
- Will AI save the world or end it?
