# Module 1: Introduction to Informatics 🌐

**Week 1 | Data, Technology & Society**

---

## Welcome to INF 110!

This module is your introduction to the course, the syllabus, the Canvas site, assignments, and each other! We'll also begin exploring the fundamental question: *What is informatics?*

### 🎯 What You'll Learn

- Figure out the Canvas environment and begin to feel comfortable using its functionalities
- Understand our own expectations, Dr. Bowman's expectations, and DU's expectations
- Establish our learning community for this semester
- Start thinking about Informatics and what it can mean for your future
- Consider the idea of "information" and Social Justice/DEI issues

### 🧠 Big Questions to Consider

- What exactly *is* information? Is it different from data? From knowledge?
- How does technology shape the way we communicate and think?
- What does it mean to study informatics in the 21st century?
- How do issues of diversity and equity intersect with technology?
- How is artificial intelligence changing every field that touches information?

## Module Contents

This module contains three sections:

1. **[The Informatics Landscape](what-is-informatics.md)** - What informatics is, how it differs from its sibling fields, its many subdomains, and the data-to-wisdom pyramid
2. **[Defining Information](defining-information.md)** - Buckland's three senses of information and Shannon's model of how messages move
3. **[Key Concepts from the Readings](key-concepts.md)** - Information science's big questions, Gleick on the bit, Nye on defining technology, and how ideas become machine instructions
