## What is Informatics?

Informatics sits at the intersection of three domains:

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| **Data** | Raw facts, figures, observations |
| **Systems** | Technologies that store, process, transmit information |
| **Society** | People who create, use, and are affected by information systems |

Informatics involves examining how information technologies are developed, applied, and utilized in society.

### The Data-Information-Knowledge-Wisdom Pyramid

Understanding the transformation from raw data to wisdom:

- **Data**: Discrete, objective facts about an event (Who? What? Where? When? How many?)
- **Information**: Data with analyzed relationships and connections
- **Knowledge**: Contextualized information (How?)
- **Wisdom**: Applied, reflected upon, embedded with values (Why do?)

This transformation is what enables real-world decision-making!

## Key Concepts

### What is Information Science?

According to Bawden & Robinson (2012):

> "Information science can best be understood as a field of study, with human recorded information as its concern, focusing on the components of the communication chain, studied through the perspective of domain analysis."

Information science is concerned with:
1. Computing, algorithms, and information technologies
2. Entropy in information theory and physics
3. Information recorded in documents, with meaning and knowledge

### Gleick on Information

> "Every new medium transforms the nature of human thought. In the long run, history is the story of information becoming aware of itself."

> "We can see now that information is what our world runs on: the blood and the fuel, the vital principle. It pervades the sciences from top to bottom, transforming every branch of knowledge."
